## Done
- Every check-in question shows: Nazaj (from question 2), Preskoči (only when allowed), Sporoči drugo težavo, and a red Nujna pomoč that opens the 112 screen (nothing saved).
- Preskoči is offered only on choice questions that aren't `always` (`vednoVprasaj`, stepped-up plan items) and have no emergency answer (`canSkip` in `checkin-plan.ts`).
- A skipped question is left out of `answers` (the ring shows it red) and adds a reason "Preskočeno vprašanje: …"; each skip/druga težava adds one warning level (zeleno → rumeno → rdeče), so an alert is raised.
- "Sporoči drugo težavo": one tap "Da, naj me sestra pokliče", sent with the check-in as "Sporočena druga težava – prosi za klic sestre". Can be cancelled. No free text.
