## Headline (Slovenia, heart failure)

> **Vsak peti bolnik s srčnim popuščanjem se v enem letu vrne v slovensko bolnišnico zaradi srčnega popuščanja. Vsak deseti že v 30 dneh.**
> *(One in five Slovenian heart-failure patients is back in hospital for heart failure within a year, one in ten within 30 days.)*

Source: national Slovenian data, all hospitals ✅

- **43,606** first heart-failure hospitalisations in Slovenia (adults, data for first hospitalisations 2008–2012).
- After a first heart-failure hospitalisation: **9.4 % (4,119) readmitted for heart failure within 30 days**, **21.8 % (9,499) within 1 year**.
- **18.9 %** died during that first hospitalisation; of those discharged alive, **3.9 %** died within 30 days and **18.0 %** within a year.
- Readmission rates differ by region: highest in Zasavska and Posavska, lowest in Osrednjeslovenska (30-day, 2012: 80.8 vs 31.5 per 100,000). The differences disappear after adjusting for age and comorbidities.
- Data: National Hospital Discharge Registry + National Death Registry (NIJZ), linked per person.

Citation: Omersa D, Eržen I, Lainščak M, Farkas J. *Regional differences in heart failure hospitalizations, mortality, and readmissions in Slovenia 2004–2012.* ESC Heart Fail 2019;6(5):965–974. doi:10.1002/ehf2.12488. [PMC6816070](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6816070/)

**Say it honestly**
- These are **heart-failure** readmissions only. All-cause readmissions are higher, so the figures are conservative.
- The data are from 2008–2012. It is the newest national readmission figure we found. Say "national data" and the years, not "today".
- The paper prints "43 606 (47.8%)" as the share of all HF hospitalisations; 43,606 / 156,859 is 27.8 %. Don't quote that percentage.

## Cost (Europe)

> **Bolnišnične obravnave so največji strošek srčnega popuščanja v Evropi: do 92 % vseh stroškov.**
> *(Hospital stays are the main cost of heart failure in Europe: up to 92 % of all costs.)*

Source: systematic review of 49 European studies (published 2000–2024) ✅

- Annual heart-failure cost per patient in Europe: **€613 to €22,647**, depending on the country.
- **Hospitalisations are the main cost driver: 15 % to 92 % of total heart-failure costs.**
- Remote monitoring and integrated care are named as cost-effective ways to reduce this burden.
- ⚠️ The review's table links telemonitoring with 20–40 % fewer readmissions; check which studies before quoting.
- ⚠️ "€29 billion a year in the EU (about 2 % of health spending)" was quoted from this review by a search summary only. Not checked.

Citation: Darbà J, Ascanio M, Rodríguez A, et al. *Economic burden of heart failure in Europe: a systematic review of costs and cost-effectiveness.* ESC Heart Fail 2025;12(6):4055–4068. doi:10.1002/ehf2.70017. [PMC12719840](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12719840/)
